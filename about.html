About Nexlyr

We exist to give every team
an unfair advantage

Founded in 2022, Nexlyr was born from a simple frustration: the AI landscape was exploding with tools, and smart teams were drowning in noise. We became the filter.

"The future doesn't belong to those who use AI.
It belongs to those who use it well."

We believe in intelligence compounding. The right AI stack doesn't just save time today — it learns, adapts, and multiplies your advantage every month. Our job is to make sure you're building with tools that compound, not tools that stagnate.

Our Story

How we got here

Q1 2022 · FOUNDING

Nexlyr is Born

Founded by two engineers who spent years evaluating AI tools for enterprise clients. Decided to turn their research process into a dedicated firm.

Q4 2022 · FIRST 10

First 10 Clients

Reached 10 paying clients within 6 months, largely through word-of-mouth from the first two. Hired the first full-time researcher.

2023 · SCALE

Intelligence Database Launch

Launched the internal AI tools database, formalizing our 47-point evaluation framework across 800 tools. The methodology that all our work is built on.

2024 · INTEGRATION

Engineering Practice Added

Added a dedicated integration engineering team. Clients could now go from briefing to live deployment entirely within Nexlyr, ending the gap between advice and execution.

2025 · GROWTH

300+ Clients, 12 Countries

Crossed 300 active clients across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. Launched the AI Transformation training program for enterprise teams.

2026 · NOW

The Intelligence Platform

Launching the Nexlyr platform — real-time AI tool monitoring, automated stack audits, and team-level AI readiness scoring. The next chapter begins.
