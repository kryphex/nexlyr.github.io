Common Questions

Pricing FAQ

What happens after the free trial?

After 14 days, your plan activates and the first payment is charged. We send a reminder 3 days before the trial ends. You can cancel at any time during the trial with no charge.

Can I change plans mid-cycle?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle. No fees for changing plans.

Do engineering hours roll over?

Unused engineering hours roll over for up to one month. After that they expire. Most clients use their hours in the first two weeks of each month for project sprints.

Is there a minimum commitment?

No minimum commitment on monthly plans. Annual plans are paid upfront at the discounted rate. We recommend starting monthly and moving to annual once you know the value.

What tools do you cover in evaluations?

Any tool relevant to your use case. Our database spans 80+ categories. Custom evaluations on Growth and Scale can be requested for any specific tool, including niche or enterprise-only products.

How does the money-back guarantee work?

If you're not satisfied within the first 30 days of a paid plan, we refund 100% — no questions asked. We've issued fewer than 5 refunds in our history.
